# Step 6: Content & Voice Guide

## Brand Voice Definition

### Core Voice Attributes

1. **Expert**
   - **Do**: Demonstrate deep knowledge of lighting design, installation techniques, and architectural principles
   - **Don't**: Use jargon without explanation or oversimplify complex concepts
   - **Example**: "Our architectural approach integrates lighting with your home's distinctive features, highlighting rooflines while creating balanced visual rhythm."
   - **Not**: "We put up the prettiest lights that make your house pop!"

2. **Precise**
   - **Do**: Use specific, descriptive language with exact measurements, materials, and processes
   - **Don't**: Rely on vague superlatives or generalities
   - **Example**: "Our commercial-grade LED lights feature 3000K warm white illumination with consistent color rendering across all installations."
   - **Not**: "We use the best lights that look amazing on every home."

3. **Refined**
   - **Do**: Employ sophisticated vocabulary and sentence structure while maintaining clarity
   - **Don't**: Use overly casual language, slang, or forced informality
   - **Example**: "The interplay between light and shadow creates a sophisticated ambiance that transforms your property's evening presence."
   - **Not**: "Your place will look super fancy with our awesome lights!"

4. **Confident**
   - **Do**: Make clear, direct statements about capabilities and outcomes
   - **Don't**: Use hedging language or unnecessary qualifiers
   - **Example**: "Our installation teams complete even complex projects with precision and efficiency, ensuring flawless execution."
   - **Not**: "We'll try our best to get your lights up correctly and hopefully on time."

5. **Warm**
   - **Do**: Express genuine enthusiasm and appreciation while maintaining professionalism
   - **Don't**: Be coldly technical or overly familiar
   - **Example**: "We're delighted to continue our tradition of transforming the Williams' estate for the third consecutive season."
   - **Not**: "Customer #247 has scheduled their annual installation."

### Voice Adaptation by Context

| Context | Voice Adaptation | Example |
|---------|------------------|---------|
| **Homepage** | Confident, aspirational, refined | "Transform your property into an architectural showcase with precision-designed holiday lighting." |
| **Services** | Detailed, expert, solution-oriented | "Our residential lighting design process begins with a comprehensive property assessment, identifying architectural features that will benefit from strategic illumination." |
| **Gallery** | Visual-focused, descriptive, elegant | "This Tudor estate features warm white lighting that accentuates the distinctive gables and creates a cohesive visual narrative." |
| **Process** | Clear, precise, reassuring | "Following your design approval, our installation team arrives with a detailed execution plan specific to your property." |
| **Pricing** | Transparent, value-focused, confident | "Our investment ranges reflect the comprehensive nature of our service, from custom design through maintenance and removal." |
| **About Us** | Personal, authentic, proud | "Founded on the principle that holiday lighting should enhance architectural beauty rather than distract from it, our team brings over two decades of design expertise." |
| **Blog** | Educational, conversational, insightful | "When considering lighting for contemporary homes, the clean lines and minimal ornamentation require a particularly thoughtful approach." |
| **FAQs** | Clear, helpful, direct | "We exclusively use commercial-grade LED lighting systems with a 5-year warranty, significantly outlasting retail options." |

## Holiday-Specific Language Guidelines

### Approved Terminology

| Category | Preferred Terms | Terms to Avoid |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Lighting** | Architectural lighting, custom illumination, lighting design, warm white illumination, LED technology | Christmas lights, twinkle lights, string lights, colored lights, blinking lights |
| **Installation** | Professional installation, precision mounting, architectural integration, custom design | Hanging lights, decorating, putting up lights, stringing lights |
| **Season** | Holiday season, winter display, seasonal transformation, year-end celebration | Christmas decorations, holiday decorations, festive decor |
| **Experience** | Sophisticated ambiance, architectural showcase, distinguished property, elevated experience | Winter wonderland, magical display, festive atmosphere, Christmas spirit |
| **Design** | Architectural enhancement, custom lighting plan, property-specific design, lighting composition | Holiday decorating, Christmas theme, festive look |

### Holiday Clichés to Avoid

1. **Banned Phrases**:
   - "'Tis the season"
   - "Light up the holidays"
   - "Holiday magic/magical"
   - "Deck the halls"
   - "Spread holiday cheer"
   - "Winter wonderland"
   - "Festive spirit"
   - "Merry and bright"
   - "Jingle all the way"
   - "Making spirits bright"

2. **Approach Instead**:
   - Focus on architectural enhancement
   - Emphasize sophisticated transformation
   - Discuss property distinction
   - Reference design principles
   - Highlight technical excellence

### Seasonal Sensitivity

- **Inclusive Language**: Use "holiday" rather than specific religious references
- **Timing Awareness**: Acknowledge appropriate seasonal timing (not "early" or "late")
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Recognize diverse celebrations without presumption
- **Visual Representation**: Show diverse properties, families, and celebrations
- **Calendar Awareness**: Adjust messaging based on time of year (planning vs. installation)

## Content Structure Guidelines

### Page Structure Formula

1. **Hero Section**
   - Headline: Clear value proposition (7-9 words)
   - Subheadline: Supporting benefit statement (12-15 words)
   - Visual: High-quality relevant imagery
   - CTA: Primary action button

2. **Introduction**
   - Problem/Opportunity Statement (2-3 sentences)
   - Solution Overview (3-4 sentences)
   - Unique Approach Highlight (2-3 sentences)

3. **Core Content**
   - Clear Section Headlines (benefit-focused)
   - Concise Paragraphs (3-5 sentences)
   - Bulleted Lists (when appropriate)
   - Supporting Visuals
   - Testimonial Integration

4. **Supporting Evidence**
   - Social Proof Elements
   - Statistical Validation
   - Process Explanation
   - Feature/Benefit Pairing

5. **Conclusion**
   - Summary Statement (2-3 sentences)
   - Next Steps Guidance
   - Primary and Secondary CTAs

### Paragraph Structure

- **Opening Sentence**: Main idea or benefit statement
- **Supporting Sentences**: Evidence, explanation, or elaboration (2-3 sentences)
- **Concluding Sentence**: Reinforcement or transition
- **Length**: 3-5 sentences maximum per paragraph
- **Rhythm**: Vary sentence length for readability (mix of short and medium sentences)

### Content Hierarchy

1. **Primary Content** (Must Read)
   - Headlines
   - Subheadlines
   - First sentences of paragraphs
   - Bulleted lists
   - Captions under images

2. **Secondary Content** (Should Read)
   - Body paragraphs
   - Process explanations
   - Feature descriptions
   - Testimonial quotes

3. **Tertiary Content** (Could Read)
   - Technical specifications
   - Extended descriptions
   - Additional options
   - Supplementary information

## SEO Content Integration

### Keyword Integration Guidelines

1. **Natural Placement**
   - Include primary keywords in headlines and first paragraphs
   - Incorporate secondary keywords throughout body content
   - Use semantic variations rather than exact repetition
   - Prioritize readability over keyword density

2. **Strategic Locations**
   - Page titles (H1): Include primary keyword
   - Section headings (H2, H3): Include secondary keywords
   - Image alt text: Descriptive with relevant keywords
   - Meta descriptions: Compelling summary with primary keyword
   - URL structure: Include primary keyword

3. **Content Depth**
   - Comprehensive coverage of topic (1,000+ words for service pages)
   - Answer common questions related to keywords
   - Include related concepts and terminology
   - Provide valuable information beyond basic descriptions

### Local Content Strategy

1. **Area-Specific Pages**
   - Unique content for each service area
   - Local landmarks and architectural styles mentioned
   - Area-specific testimonials and project examples
   - Community connections and local knowledge

2. **Geo-Targeted Elements**
   - Area names in headlines and subheadings
   - Local terminology and references
   - Maps and service area visualizations
   - Location-specific FAQs

3. **Neighborhood Guides**
   - Architectural styles common to area
   - Notable installations in neighborhood
   - Local regulations or HOA considerations
   - Community-specific design approaches

## Editorial Guidelines

### Grammar & Style

1. **Tense & Voice**
   - Present tense for capabilities and offerings
   - Active voice in most contexts
   - First-person plural ("we") for company perspective
   - Second-person ("you") for client-directed content

2. **Punctuation**
   - Oxford comma in all lists
   - Em dashes for emphasis and asides
   - Minimal exclamation points (reserve for genuine enthusiasm)
   - Periods inside quotation marks

3. **Formatting**
   - Bold for emphasis of key points
   - Italics for publication titles and specialized terms
   - Bulleted lists for features and benefits
   - Numbered lists for sequential processes

4. **Numbers & Units**
   - Spell out numbers one through nine
   - Use numerals for 10 and above
   - Use numerals for all measurements
   - Include units of measurement (feet, inches, etc.)

### Word Choice & Terminology

1. **Industry Terminology**
   - Use proper lighting design terms
   - Define technical terms when first introduced
   - Maintain consistency in terminology throughout
   - Avoid unnecessary jargon

2. **Descriptive Language**
   - Use specific, vivid adjectives
   - Employ architectural terminology correctly
   - Choose precise verbs over generic ones
   - Balance technical and accessible language

3. **Benefit-Focused Language**
   - Connect features to benefits
   - Emphasize outcomes over processes
   - Focus on client experience
   - Highlight emotional and practical value

4. **Persuasive Elements**
   - Use social proof strategically
   - Incorporate subtle scarcity when relevant
   - Emphasize exclusivity appropriately
   - Balance emotion and logic
