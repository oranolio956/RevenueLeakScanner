# Step 13: Gallery Shot List

## Photography Direction Overview

### Visual Style Guidelines

1. **Overall Aesthetic**
   - **Clean & Architectural**: Focus on clean lines, architectural features, and thoughtful composition
   - **Sophisticated & Refined**: Avoid overly festive or whimsical elements
   - **Editorial Quality**: Professional, magazine-worthy photography
   - **Consistent Look**: Cohesive visual style across all property types
   - **Authentic**: Real properties and installations, never stock photography

2. **Lighting & Exposure**
   - **Blue Hour Priority**: Shoot during "blue hour" (15-30 minutes after sunset) for optimal contrast
   - **Balanced Exposure**: Properly expose for both lighting and architecture
   - **Avoid Overexposure**: Maintain detail in the lighting elements
   - **Natural Ambiance**: Capture the natural glow rather than artificially brightening
   - **Interior Context**: When applicable, capture warm interior lighting visible through windows

3. **Color Treatment**
   - **Rich, Slightly Desaturated**: Avoid overly vibrant processing
   - **Warm White Emphasis**: Showcase the consistent 2700K-3000K lighting temperature
   - **Complementary Sky Tones**: Preserve deep blue evening sky when present
   - **Subtle Contrast**: Enhance architectural details without appearing artificial
   - **Consistent White Balance**: Maintain consistent color across all images

4. **Composition Guidelines**
   - **Architectural Focus**: Highlight how lighting enhances architectural features
   - **Rule of Thirds**: Apply classical composition principles
   - **Leading Lines**: Use property lines and lighting to guide the viewer's eye
   - **Balanced Framing**: Include appropriate context and surrounding elements
   - **Consistent Horizon**: Maintain level horizon across all images
   - **Multiple Perspectives**: Capture each property from various angles and distances

## Required Shot Categories

### 1. Hero/Wide Establishing Shots

**Purpose**: Showcase the complete property transformation and overall lighting design

**Technical Requirements**:
- Resolution: Minimum 2560 × 1440px (16:9 aspect ratio)
- Composition: Wide angle showing entire property facade
- Timing: Prime blue hour (15-20 minutes after sunset)
- Equipment: Tripod required, wide-angle lens (16-24mm equivalent)

**Shot List**:

1. **Front Elevation - Straight On**
   - Full property view from direct front perspective
   - Include complete roofline, entry, and front landscaping
   - Capture property within its environment
   - Ensure vertical lines remain straight (correct perspective distortion)

2. **Front Elevation - Angled (45°)**
   - Three-quarter view showing depth and dimension
   - Capture multiple facades when possible
   - Include landscaping and entry path
   - Frame to show relationship between lighting elements

3. **Property Approach**
   - View from street or driveway approach
   - Capture the arrival experience
   - Include entry features (gates, columns, etc.)
   - Show how lighting guides the eye to the property

4. **Rear Elevation** (when applicable)
   - Complete view of rear facade
   - Include outdoor living spaces
   - Capture relationship between interior and exterior lighting
   - Show how lighting enhances backyard features

5. **Aerial/Elevated Perspective** (when possible)
   - Overhead view showing complete property
   - Capture relationship between all lighting elements
   - Include context of surrounding environment
   - Highlight overall lighting design cohesion

**Example Direction**:
"Capture the complete front elevation of this Tudor estate, ensuring the entire roofline and facade are visible. Position to show how the lighting accentuates the distinctive gables and timber detailing. Shoot during peak blue hour to balance the warm lighting against the deep blue sky. Include the landscape lighting that frames the entry approach."

## Specific Shot Requirements

### Technical Specifications

1. **File Formats & Resolution**
   - RAW format for all original files
   - Processed JPEGs at highest quality
   - Minimum resolution requirements:
     * Hero Images: 2560 × 1440px (16:9)
     * Gallery Images: 1920 × 1080px (16:9)
     * Detail Shots: 1200 × 1200px (1:1)
     * Team/Process Photos: 1200 × 800px (3:2)
   - Provide both high-resolution files and web-optimized versions

2. **Equipment Requirements**
   - Full-frame camera preferred
   - Tripod required for all evening shots
   - Wide-angle lens (16-24mm equivalent)
   - Standard zoom lens (24-70mm equivalent)
   - Optional telephoto for details (70-200mm equivalent)
   - Remote trigger for long exposures
   - Polarizing filter for daytime shots

3. **Exposure Guidelines**
   - Bracket exposures (minimum 3 stops)
   - Capture for HDR when necessary (extreme contrast)
   - Base exposure to properly show lighting detail
   - ISO priority: keep below 800 when possible
   - Long exposure for smooth sky transitions
   - Consistent aperture for depth of field (f/8-f/11)

4. **Post-Processing Direction**
   - Natural, editorial style
   - Subtle HDR when necessary (avoid over-processing)
   - Consistent color temperature across portfolio
   - Vertical correction for architecture
   - Remove distractions (temporary signs, vehicles)
   - Maintain authentic appearance of lighting

### Shot Planning Checklist

1. **Pre-Shoot Planning**
   - Scout locations during daylight
   - Identify optimal camera positions
   - Note sun/light direction for timing
   - Secure necessary permissions
   - Check weather forecast
   - Coordinate with installation team

2. **Equipment Checklist**
   - Camera body and backup
   - Lens selection
   - Fully charged batteries (extras)
   - Empty memory cards
   - Tripod and head
   - Remote trigger
   - Filters (polarizer, ND)
   - Lens cleaning supplies
   - Light modifiers if needed

3. **Timing Schedule**
   - Arrive 30-45 minutes before sunset
   - Capture establishing shots during golden hour
   - Priority shots during blue hour (15-30 min after sunset)
   - Detail shots after full darkness
   - Plan for 1.5-2 hours per property

4. **On-Site Workflow**
   - Exterior overview shots first
   - Key architectural features during prime light
   - Detail shots after dark
   - Multiple compositions of key features
   - Verify image quality on-site
   - Document property details for captioning

5. **Delivery Requirements**
   - Organized by property and shot type
   - Consistent file naming convention
   - Complete metadata (location, date, property type)
   - Both high-res and web-optimized versions
   - Before/after pairs clearly marked
   - Delivery within 5 business days of shoot
