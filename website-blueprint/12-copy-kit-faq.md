Third, our comprehensive service experience includes professional design, meticulous installation, proactive maintenance, and careful removal—delivering exceptional results without any effort or concern on your part.

### Design Process

**Q: What happens during the design consultation?**

A: Our design consultation is a comprehensive assessment of your property and vision. A certified lighting designer will visit your property to analyze its architectural features, discuss your aesthetic preferences, and understand how you use your outdoor spaces during winter. We'll take measurements, photographs, and notes to develop your custom lighting plan. For larger properties, we create digital renderings or 3D visualizations to illustrate the proposed design. The consultation typically takes 60-90 minutes and is complimentary with no obligation.

**Q: Can you match a specific design I've seen elsewhere?**

A: While we can draw inspiration from designs you admire, our approach focuses on creating a custom lighting plan that enhances your property's unique architecture. Rather than replicating another property's design, we apply similar techniques and concepts in a way that complements your specific architectural features. During your consultation, we encourage you to share images of designs you like, which helps us understand your aesthetic preferences.

**Q: Do you offer different lighting colors and styles?**

A: We specialize in warm white lighting (2700K-3000K) for its sophisticated appearance and architectural enhancement qualities. This consistent color temperature creates a refined, elegant look that complements rather than competes with your property's architecture. For commercial clients with specific branding requirements, we can incorporate limited accent colors. Our design approach emphasizes quality of light, thoughtful placement, and architectural integration rather than multi-colored or animated displays.

**Q: Can you work with my existing decorations?**

A: Yes, we can integrate existing decorations that meet our quality standards into your new lighting design. During your consultation, we'll assess your current items and recommend which pieces complement our architectural approach. For cohesive results, we may suggest retiring certain elements that detract from the overall design. We can also create a phased plan to gradually transition to a complete architectural lighting design over multiple seasons.

**Q: Do you provide design services without installation?**

A: Our design and installation services are integrated to ensure the highest quality results. The detailed knowledge our designers gain during the consultation and planning process directly informs our installation techniques. While we don't offer design-only services for general consumers, we do provide consulting services for select commercial properties with in-house installation teams, as well as professional design collaboration with architects and landscape designers.

### Installation & Safety

**Q: What type of lighting do you use?**

A: We exclusively use commercial-grade LED lighting systems with warm white color temperature (2700K-3000K). These professional-quality lights offer superior brightness, consistency, and durability compared to retail options. Our lighting components feature commercial-grade wiring, weather-resistant connections, and professional mounting systems designed for winter conditions. All our products carry a 5-year warranty and are UL-listed for outdoor use, with energy-efficient operation that minimizes electricity costs.

**Q: How do you attach lights without damaging my property?**

A: Our installation methods prioritize both aesthetic results and property protection. We use a variety of specialized mounting techniques appropriate for different surfaces and materials, including non-invasive clips for gutters and rooflines, temporary adhesives for certain surfaces, and specialized hardware for specific applications. Our technicians are trained to assess each area and select the appropriate method that secures the lighting without causing damage. For unique architectural features, we develop custom mounting solutions that protect your property while achieving the desired lighting effect.

**Q: Are your lights energy efficient?**

A: Yes, all our lighting systems use LED technology, consuming approximately 80% less electricity than traditional incandescent lights. A typical residential installation adds only $30-60 to your monthly electricity bill during the display season. For larger installations, we can provide specific energy consumption estimates based on your design. We also offer optional timers and smart controls to further optimize energy usage by automatically managing display hours.

**Q: What happens if there's a storm or severe weather?**

A: Our installations are designed and tested to withstand typical winter weather conditions, including snow, ice, and moderate winds. All components are rated for outdoor use with commercial-grade connections and mounting systems. In the event of extreme weather, our Storm Check service provides priority response to inspect and repair any weather-related issues. For clients with our premium service packages, these inspections are included automatically after severe weather events.

**Q: How are the lights powered?**

A: We carefully plan power requirements during the design phase, utilizing your existing exterior outlets when possible and recommending additional electrical work if necessary. For most residential installations, standard exterior outlets are sufficient when strategically utilized. Our systems include commercial-grade extension cords that are properly rated, secured, and concealed. For larger installations, we may recommend dedicated circuits or temporary power solutions. All electrical connections are protected from moisture with commercial-grade covers and proper placement.
