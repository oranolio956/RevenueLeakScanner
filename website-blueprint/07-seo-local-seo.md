## URL Structure

### URL Pattern Guidelines

1. **Homepage**: `domain.com`
2. **Service Pages**: `domain.com/services/[service-name]`
3. **Location Pages**: `domain.com/locations/[city-name]`
4. **Blog Articles**: `domain.com/blog/[category]/[post-name]`
5. **Gallery Pages**: `domain.com/gallery/[category]/[project-name]`
6. **Process Pages**: `domain.com/process/[stage-name]`

### URL Best Practices

- Use hyphens to separate words
- Keep URLs under 60 characters when possible
- Include primary keyword in URL
- Avoid unnecessary parameters or numbers
- Use lowercase letters only
- Avoid special characters
- Create logical hierarchy
- Implement proper redirects for any URL changes

## Meta Data Framework

### Title Tag Formula

1. **Homepage**: `[Brand Name] | [Primary Service] in [Primary Location]`
   - Example: "Illuminate Distinction | Premium Holiday Lighting in Chicago's North Shore"

2. **Service Pages**: `[Specific Service] in [Location] | [Brand Name]`
   - Example: "Residential Holiday Lighting in Lake Forest | Illuminate Distinction"

3. **Location Pages**: `[Primary Service] in [Location] | [Brand Name]`
   - Example: "Professional Holiday Lighting in Winnetka | Illuminate Distinction"

4. **Blog Articles**: `[Article Title] | [Brand Name]`
   - Example: "Architectural Lighting Design for Modern Homes | Illuminate Distinction"

5. **Gallery Pages**: `[Project Type] Gallery | [Location] | [Brand Name]`
   - Example: "Luxury Estate Holiday Lighting Gallery | North Shore | Illuminate Distinction"

### Meta Description Formula

1. **Homepage**: `[Unique Value Proposition] for [Target Audience]. [Key Benefit] with [Service Highlight]. Serving [Locations].`
   - Example: "Premium architectural holiday lighting for distinguished properties. Transform your home with custom design and flawless installation. Serving Chicago's North Shore and surrounding areas."

2. **Service Pages**: `[Service Description] for [Target Audience] in [Location]. [Key Benefit] and [Unique Feature]. [Call to Action].`
   - Example: "Expert residential holiday lighting for luxury homes in Lake Forest. Custom architectural designs and white-glove installation service. Schedule your consultation today."

3. **Location Pages**: `[Primary Service] in [Location] by [Brand Name]. [Local Relevance] with [Service Highlight]. [Call to Action].`
   - Example: "Professional holiday lighting in Winnetka by Illuminate Distinction. Familiar with local architectural styles and HOA requirements. Book your design consultation now."

4. **Blog Articles**: `[Article Summary] [Key Insight]. [Benefit to Reader].`
   - Example: "Discover architectural lighting techniques for modern homes. Learn how to enhance clean lines and contemporary features with sophisticated holiday lighting design."

5. **Gallery Pages**: `View our [Project Type] in [Location]. [Highlight Feature] and [Unique Aspect]. [Call to Action].`
   - Example: "View our luxury estate holiday lighting in North Shore. Featuring custom architectural integration and signature warm white illumination. Find inspiration for your property."

### Meta Data Character Limits

- **Title Tags**: 55-60 characters maximum
- **Meta Descriptions**: 145-155 characters maximum
- **H1 Headlines**: 40-60 characters recommended
- **H2 Subheadings**: 50-70 characters recommended

## Alt Text Conventions

### Alt Text Formula

1. **Gallery Images**: `[Property Type] with [Lighting Style] in [Location] by [Brand Name]`
   - Example: "Tudor estate with architectural roofline lighting in Lake Forest by Illuminate Distinction"

2. **Service Images**: `[Service Name] showing [Key Feature] for [Property Type]`
   - Example: "Professional installation service showing precise roofline mounting for colonial home"

3. **Team Photos**: `[Name], [Title] at [Brand Name] [Action if applicable]`
   - Example: "Michael Johnson, Lead Designer at Illuminate Distinction reviewing lighting plans"

4. **Process Images**: `[Process Stage] showing [Specific Activity] for [Result]`
   - Example: "Design consultation showing 3D modeling for custom lighting plan"

5. **Before/After Images**: `[Before/After] of [Property Type] [Lighting Transformation] in [Location]`
   - Example: "Before and after of Mediterranean villa lighting transformation in Highland Park"

### Alt Text Best Practices

- Keep under 125 characters
- Be specific and descriptive
- Include relevant keywords naturally
- Describe the image content accurately
- Include location when relevant
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Provide context for the image
- Use proper capitalization and punctuation

## Internal Linking Strategy

### Link Architecture

1. **Hub and Spoke Model**
   - Core service pages serve as hubs
   - Related content (blog posts, gallery items, FAQs) serve as spokes
   - Each hub links to all related spokes
   - Spokes link back to their hub and to 1-2 related spokes

2. **Hierarchical Structure**
   - Homepage links to all main category pages
   - Category pages link to all subcategory pages
   - Subcategory pages link to individual service/content pages
   - Bottom-level pages link up to parent categories

3. **Cross-Service Connections**
   - Related services linked together
   - Natural progression paths (design → installation → maintenance)
   - Complementary service suggestions

### Anchor Text Strategy

| Link Type | Anchor Text Approach | Example |
|-----------|----------------------|---------|
| **Primary Navigation** | Clear, concise service names | "Residential Lighting" |
| **Secondary Navigation** | Descriptive phrases | "Learn About Our Process" |
| **In-Content Links** | Natural, varied phrasing | "our custom design approach", "professional installation services" |
| **Related Content** | Specific, descriptive | "View Tudor Estate Project Gallery" |
| **Call to Action** | Action-oriented | "Schedule Your Design Consultation" |
| **Footer Links** | Concise keywords | "Commercial Services", "Design Process" |

### Internal Linking Best Practices

1. **Quantity Guidelines**
   - Homepage: Links to all main sections
   - Service Pages: 5-7 internal links minimum
   - Blog Posts: 3-5 contextual internal links
   - Gallery Items: Links to related projects and services
   - Location Pages: Links to services available in that area

2. **Link Placement**
   - Include links in first paragraph when relevant
   - Use contextual links within body content
   - Add "related services" section at bottom of pages
   - Include "next steps" links at logical progression points
   - Use sidebar/widget areas for additional relevant links

3. **Technical Considerations**
   - Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text
   - Vary anchor text naturally
   - Avoid generic "click here" or "read more" text
   - Ensure all links are functional and relevant
   - Use absolute URLs for critical pages

## Mobile SEO Considerations

### Mobile-First Optimization

1. **Page Speed Optimization**
   - Compressed images with WebP format
   - Lazy loading for off-screen images
   - Minified CSS and JavaScript
   - Browser caching implementation
   - Critical CSS delivery
   - Reduced server response time
   - Optimized font loading

2. **Mobile Layout Priorities**
   - Simplified navigation with clear hierarchy
   - Touch-friendly buttons (minimum 44×44px)
   - Adequate spacing between interactive elements
   - Readable font sizes (minimum 16px)
   - Visible search functionality
   - Click-to-call phone numbers
   - Mobile-optimized forms

3. **Content Adaptation**
   - Prioritized content for mobile users
   - Collapsed sections for secondary content
   - Simplified tables and data presentation
   - Optimized image sizes for mobile screens
   - Reduced form fields on mobile
   - Location-aware functionality

### Core Web Vitals Optimization

1. **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)**
   - Target: Under 2.5 seconds
   - Optimize largest page elements
   - Implement preloading for critical resources
   - Optimize image delivery
   - Use content delivery network (CDN)

2. **First Input Delay (FID)**
   - Target: Under 100ms
   - Minimize JavaScript execution time
   - Break up long tasks
   - Optimize event handlers
   - Implement lazy loading for non-critical JS

3. **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)**
   - Target: Under 0.1
   - Set explicit dimensions for images and embeds
   - Reserve space for dynamic content
   - Avoid inserting content above existing content
   - Optimize font loading to prevent shifts

4. **Additional Metrics**
   - Time to Interactive (TTI): Under 3.8 seconds
   - Total Blocking Time (TBT): Under 200ms
   - Speed Index (SI): Under 3.4 seconds

## Technical SEO Elements

### XML Sitemap Configuration

1. **Structure**
   - Hierarchical organization matching site structure
   - Separate sitemaps for different content types
   - Index sitemap linking to all sub-sitemaps
   - Priority settings based on page importance

2. **Inclusion Rules**
   - Include all indexable pages
   - Exclude non-canonical URLs
   - Exclude login/admin pages
   - Exclude duplicate content pages
   - Exclude low-value utility pages

3. **Update Frequency**
   - Homepage: daily
   - Service pages: weekly
   - Blog posts: monthly
   - Gallery: weekly
   - Automatically generate after content updates

4. **Technical Specifications**
   - Valid XML format
   - Proper namespace declarations
   - Compressed for faster processing
   - Submitted to Google Search Console
   - Referenced in robots.txt

### Canonical URL Implementation

1. **Self-Referencing Canonicals**
   - Every page includes canonical tag pointing to itself
   - Prevents duplicate content issues from parameters
   - Handles session IDs and tracking parameters

2. **Pagination Handling**
   - Implement rel="next" and rel="prev" for paginated content
   - Set canonical to first page or view-all page as appropriate
   - Consistent URL structure for paginated content

3. **Cross-Domain Canonicals**
   - Used for syndicated content when necessary
   - Points to original source on main domain
   - Prevents duplicate content penalties

4. **Technical Implementation**
   - Placed in `<head>` section
   - Uses absolute URLs
   - Consistent across all pages
   - Properly handles HTTPS vs HTTP

1. **Global Implementations**
   - Organization schema on all pages

   - BreadcrumbList schema on all pages
   - WebSite schema on homepage

2. **Page-Specific Implementations**
   - Service pages: Service schema
   - Blog posts: Article schema
